# Design

## Direction

Premium minimal, modern, calm, and content-first. Preserve familiar Obsidian behavior and Markdown portability.

## Principles

- Clear hierarchy and standardized spacing.
- One restrained accent plus literal status colors.
- Readable typography and practical information density.
- Visible keyboard focus and WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- No decorative AI clichés, plugin bloat, or interface novelty without functional value.
