Route business operations, organizations, clients, people, products, meetings, and strategy.

## Subdomain Map

- `Clients/`: client-specific records; access may be restricted.
- `Companies/`: organization profiles.
- `People/`: role and relationship context; avoid unnecessary personal data.
- `Meetings/`: agendas, decisions, and follow-ups.
- `Operations/`: repeatable operating procedures.
- `Products/`: product context and lifecycle.
- `Strategy/`: durable strategic direction.

## Active Context

Link active work without duplicating project handoffs.

## Sensitive Areas

Apply `{{business_ai_access}}` access. Client, employee, finance, legal, and credential material require explicit handling.

## Archive Guidance

Archive inactive records only after preserving ownership, decisions, and retention requirements.
