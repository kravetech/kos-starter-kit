# Projects Map of Content

## Purpose

Route initiatives through Incubating, Active, On-Hold, Completed, and Archive states.

## Start Here

- `Active/`
- `Incubating/`
- `On-Hold/`

## Project Context Rule

Create `PROJECT-CONTEXT.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `memory.md`, and `handoff.md` only when a project becomes active or complex enough to require persistent continuity.

## Lifecycle

`Idea -> Incubating -> Active -> On-Hold or Completed -> Archive`

## Archive Guidance

Preserve decisions and a final handoff before archival. Do not keep completed execution history in active handoff files.
